# Krineo — Brand Messaging Architecture

**The verbal identity and messaging system for accountable AI market reasoning.**

## Executive diagnosis

Krineo should not present itself as another AI trading assistant. That category is crowded with products that generate signals, charts or confident-looking answers. The stronger and more ownable territory is **accountable market reasoning**: showing how evidence becomes a thesis, how alternatives were rejected, how a challenge was handled and why a later decision changed.

Krineo’s strongest asset is its **versioned reasoning trail**. Its biggest communication risk is feature sprawl. The product can be mistaken for a signal bot, paper-trading game, charting dashboard, social sentiment monitor, generic LLM interface or multi-agent trading experiment.

The message system therefore follows one rule:

> **Build the evidence-to-decision loop first. Let every message prove or support that loop.**

> **Current implementation disclosure:** Shipped Krineo can run bounded research through LIVE RYO or clearly labeled REPLAY, then apply deterministic policy and optional simulated practice. The current Golden Demo is fixture-backed. “Live,” “agent,” “persistent,” and “autonomous” must be qualified by provider availability and the current no-database/no-execution boundary.

Krineo is not a brokerage, exchange, custody layer, autonomous fund or real-money trading product. Its core output is an evidence-backed market thesis, including the disciplined decision not to trade.

**Unaccountable conviction**:

- One-source decisions presented as certainty.
- Missing data quietly turned into neutral data.
- Model explanations that change without a versioned state.
- Performance screenshots treated as proof of reasoning quality.
- Social opinion treated as evidence.

### Core problem

Market research tools provide data and AI agents provide explanations, but users often cannot see how a conclusion was assembled, what contradicted it, what alternatives were rejected or what would invalidate it.

### Primary promise

> **Krineo gives every market thesis an evidence trail, an adversarial review and a versioned record of what changed.**

### Difference

Krineo joins four mechanisms into one accountability loop:

1. **Opportunity Cost** compares the target with realistic alternatives and `ABSTAIN`.
2. **KillSwitch** challenges the provisional thesis before commitment.
3. **Thesis Receipt** preserves the exact structured state and its provenance.
4. **Strategy Diff** shows the evidence-level cause of a later change.

A product that only generates a chat explanation is not equivalent.

The proof must be product behaviour, not adjectives:

- RYO evidence calls are read-only and provenance-aware.
- Raw provider responses are preserved before normalisation.
- DM-1 is deterministic and versioned.
- Unknown evidence lowers coverage rather than becoming neutral.
- Rejected alternatives remain visible.
- Committed versions are append-only.
- KillSwitch cannot arbitrarily edit a score.
- Refresh creates a new version instead of replacing the old one.
- Practice positions are fixed and clearly simulated.
- The product can show an evidence change causing a visible decision change.

### Full narrative

AI has made it easier to produce a market opinion and harder to tell whether that opinion deserves trust. A confident paragraph can hide weak coverage, conflicting evidence, a missing source or a conclusion that would have been different yesterday.

Krineo treats a market decision as a recordable reasoning event. It researches through RYO, preserves source and freshness, compares realistic alternatives and includes `ABSTAIN` in the decision set. Before commitment, KillSwitch looks for the strongest supported objection. If the thesis survives, Krineo commits a Thesis Receipt containing the structured evidence, policy version, contradictions, alternatives and invalidation conditions.

Later, the receipt does not disappear and the model does not quietly rewrite history. A refresh creates a new version. Strategy Diff shows what changed, how the evidence moved and whether the original thesis weakened or became invalid.

The result is not a promise that the market can be predicted. It is a better standard for AI-assisted judgement: clear enough to act on, honest enough to abstain and accountable enough to revisit.

## Strategic narrative

### The promised land

A market-reasoning environment where an AI can help form a clear thesis without pretending to eliminate uncertainty; where every commitment has a receipt; and where changing one’s mind is credible because the cause is visible.

## Verbal identity

### Voice

Krineo is the calm analyst who shows the work and the rigorous reviewer who is willing to say “unknown.” It is technically capable without being theatrical, direct without being reckless and confident in process without claiming certainty in the market.

### Voice principles

#### 1. Show the work

**Meaning:** Explain the evidence path before celebrating the conclusion.

- **Sounds like:** “SOL leads by 7 points because momentum and market alignment remain supportive. Safety is available; narrative is unknown.”
- **Does not sound like:** “SOL is the obvious winner.”

#### 2. Be decisive about uncertainty

**Meaning:** Unknown is a valid state with a consequence.

- **Sounds like:** “We cannot establish opportunity cost because comparison timed out. Result: `ABSTAIN`.”
- **Does not sound like:** “The market looks neutral for now.”

#### 3. Use plain language for technical truth

**Meaning:** Expose policy without forcing the user to understand implementation jargon.

- **Sounds like:** “The thesis did not clear the coverage threshold.”
- **Does not sound like:** “The latent evidence vector failed its weighted aggregation.”

#### 4. Challenge without theatre

**Meaning:** A challenge identifies a claim and the evidence that supports or fails it.

- **Sounds like:** “The thesis depends on one evidence family. Remove it and the decision becomes `ABSTAIN`.”
- **Does not sound like:** “KillSwitch destroyed the bull case.”

#### 5. Never turn simulation into certainty

**Meaning:** Practice positions are for learning and observation, not proof.

- **Sounds like:** “Practice position opened at $1,000 notional. P&L is context, not proof.”
- **Does not sound like:** “The strategy made money, so the agent was right.”

### Tone by context

| Context | Tone | Example |
|---|---|---|
| First visit | Clear and inviting | “Start with a market question. We’ll show the evidence before the conclusion.” |
| Researching | Calm and transparent | “Comparing four candidates. Safety checks are still running.” |
| Strong decision | Precise, not triumphant | “`LONG` cleared the directional threshold with 86% coverage and moderate conflict.” |
| `ABSTAIN` | Reassuring and firm | “No candidate cleared the policy. `ABSTAIN` is the result.” |
| KillSwitch | Analytical | “One challenge is supported: the thesis is fragile if momentum evidence is removed.” |
| Veto | Serious and explanatory | “`VETO`: required safety evidence is unavailable. No thesis was committed.” |
| Refresh | Curious and causal | “New evidence changed regime fit. Here is the effect on the thesis.” |
| Invalidation | Direct, non-dramatic | “The precommitted direction-loss rule fired. The thesis is now invalidated.” |
| Social challenge | Respectful and testable | “We turned that counterclaim into a research question.” |
| Simulated result | Humble | “Practice P&L is shown for context. It does not validate the strategy.” |
| Error | Honest and useful | “RYO comparison timed out. We preserved the failure and returned `ABSTAIN` because opportunity cost is unknown.” |

### Cadence

- Short declarative sentences for state changes.
- One idea per line in evidence views.
- Compact labels and explicit status values.
- Longer explanation only when connecting cause to effect.
- Use a pause before a decision: `Evidence changed. Decision changed.`
- Avoid stacking three abstract nouns where one concrete verb will do.

### Why this direction?

> Momentum and technical confluence support the direction. Relative opportunity is stronger than the alternatives by 7 points. Market alignment is supportive. Narrative is currently unknown and does not add directional weight.

### What worries us?

> Conflict is moderate. The thesis is sensitive to the momentum evidence family. If regime fit deteriorates or coverage falls below policy, the thesis will weaken or invalidate.

### Alternatives

**Intro**

> Before commitment, KillSwitch looks for the strongest supported reason this thesis could be wrong.

**Clear**

> `CLEAR` — no material supported challenge changed the policy result.

**Caution**

> `CAUTION` — a supported concern was found, but the thesis still clears policy. Review the affected evidence before committing.

**Veto**

> `VETO` — a hard rule failed. This thesis cannot be committed in its current state.

**Unknown**

> `UNKNOWN` — a critical challenge could not be evaluated because required evidence was missing or unavailable.

### Thesis Receipt

**Header**

> `THESIS RECEIPT · VERSION 1`

**Descriptor**

> The evidence and policy state committed at this moment. Later updates create new versions.

**Integrity copy**

> This receipt records structured state, not a guarantee of future returns.

**Share prompt**

> Share the reasoning, not just the result.

### Strategy Diff

**Header**

> What changed?

**Intro**

> Compare the original evidence state with the latest refresh. Krineo does not rewrite the old receipt.

**Causal result**

> Regime fit changed from supportive to neutral. Directional score fell below threshold. The precommitted direction-loss rule fired, so the thesis is now weakened.

**Invalidation**

> The thesis is invalidated because a precommitted hard gate failed. The original receipt remains unchanged.

### Failure and degraded states

**Optional provider unavailable**

> Narrative research is unavailable. The thesis can continue without it, but Narrative Gap is `UNKNOWN` and coverage is shown as partial where relevant.

**Required evidence unavailable**

> Required safety evidence is unavailable. Krineo will not manufacture a directional result. `ABSTAIN`.

**Comparison unavailable**

> Opportunity cost cannot be established because comparison failed. `ABSTAIN / NO_CLEAR_WINNER`.

**AI provider unavailable**

> The deterministic evidence state is preserved. Explanation and challenge generation are temporarily limited.

**Stale evidence**

> This evidence is cached and stale as of `[timestamp]`. It remains visible but cannot be presented as current.

|---|---|
| “Is this a trading bot?” | No. Krineo researches and reasons about market theses. RYO research is read-only and practice positions are simulated. |
| “Is this financial advice?” | No. Krineo is an evidence and decision-reasoning tool. It does not guarantee returns or remove the need for human judgement. |
| “Why not use a charting terminal?” | Charts show movement. Krineo organises the decision: evidence, alternatives, challenge, commitment and change. |
| “Why does the receipt matter?” | A result without its original reasoning is easy to rewrite with hindsight. The receipt preserves what was believed and why. |
| “Why include `ABSTAIN`?” | Because forcing a direction from weak or conflicting evidence is not intelligence. `ABSTAIN` is a valid policy result. |
| “Why not let the model decide?” | Models explain and synthesise. Rules decide the policy outcome so missing data, gates and thresholds remain reproducible. |
| “Does a good paper result prove it works?” | No. P&L is contextual. Krineo evaluates reasoning transparency and evidence discipline, not a short-term return claim. |
| “What if a source fails?” | The system preserves the failure, labels the evidence unavailable or unknown and abstains when a required input cannot be established. |
| “Can I challenge a thesis?” | Yes. A challenge becomes a researched, testable claim. Popularity alone cannot change the decision. |
| “Does it rewrite the thesis when the market moves?” | No. Refresh creates a new version and Strategy Diff shows the causal evidence change. |

## Claims discipline

### Krineo may claim

- It organises and evaluates evidence.
- It preserves decision state.
- It compares alternatives.
- It exposes contradictions and missing data.
- It applies a deterministic strategy policy.
- It runs structured adversarial review.
- It creates simulated practice positions.
- It shows what changed between thesis versions.
- It provides a reusable reasoning pattern for RYO-based agents.

### Krineo must not claim

- Guaranteed returns.
- Accurate predictions.
- A profitable strategy.
- Financial advice.
- Autonomous execution.
- That P&L proves reasoning quality.
- That the model “knows” the market.
- That `LONG` or `SHORT` is a command to transact.
- That data is complete when a provider failed or a source is stale.
- That a challenge is valid merely because a user submitted it.
- That Krineo is certified, audited, trusted by named customers or legally cleared unless those claims are separately verified.

### Required qualification language

Use this near product demonstrations and practice-position views:

> **Read-only research and simulated practice positions. Not financial advice. Thesis Strength measures evidence coherence under the active strategy; it is not a prediction of future returns.**

## Messaging tests

### Five-second clarity test

A stranger should understand within five seconds:

> **Krineo helps an AI form and defend a market thesis, and shows what changed.**

If they describe it as a trading bot, charting app or social feed, rewrite the first screen.

### Repeatability test

Ask a user to explain Krineo after one exposure. They should be able to repeat at least three of these terms accurately:

- Market reasoning.
- Evidence trail.
- Opportunity Cost.
- KillSwitch.
- Thesis Receipt.
- Strategy Diff.
- `ABSTAIN`.

### Message quality tests

| Test | Question |
|---|---|
| **Appeal** | Does the promise matter to a researcher who distrusts unexplained AI outputs? |
| **Exclusivity** | Could a generic signal bot say the same thing without changing its product? |
| **Clarity** | Can the category and outcome be understood without decoding jargon? |
| **Credibility** | Does the product show the proof rather than merely assert it? |
| **So what?** | Can every feature be connected to a user outcome? |
| **Only** | Is Krineo’s combination of comparison, challenge, receipts and diff meaningfully ownable? |
| **Say/do** | Do users inspect, revisit and share receipts—not merely say they like the idea? |

### What to test with real users

- Whether “market reasoning” is understood without explanation.
- Whether “Decide with receipts” creates curiosity rather than confusion.
- Whether users understand `ABSTAIN` as intelligence rather than failure.
- Whether the Thesis Receipt is valuable enough to revisit or share.
- Whether Strategy Diff makes a changed decision feel credible.
- Whether the product sounds like research rather than financial advice.
- Whether users can identify why the selected alternative won.

## Final recommendation

Use **Krineo — Decide with receipts.** as the master verbal system.

Lead with the category before the cleverness. Make the first message about a decision that can be inspected, not about AI, crypto or profitability. Let every feature earn its place by proving one part of the accountability loop.

```text
LIVE / REPLAY-LABELED EVIDENCE
→ ALTERNATIVES

→ DECISION
→ RECEIPT

```

## Da Vinci verdict

### **Strategically strong, if kept disciplined**

- **Strongest verbal asset:** `Decide with receipts.` It is concise, ownable in spirit and connected to a real product artifact.

- **Highest-leverage change:** Put the receipt and the evidence-to-decision path at the centre of every important message.
